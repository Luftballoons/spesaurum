Who are we?

Spes Aurum's motto is to Love All, Serve All. Our future is being written by today's youth, and our work serves to provide them with the skills and experience through volunteer opportunities in various fields. We are mainly prioritising the education sector, and our Sustainable Development Goals cover Quality Education and Reduced Inequalities. These services are meant to provide orphanages, schools, children's hospitals and et cetera with equal opportunity to learn and eventually make a difference in our society.

Workshops and Activities

At our CCA "Illuminate" by SPES AURUM, we are committed to giving back to the community while providing meaningful experiences for our members. Over the past year, we have organized several visits to local orphanages, where students actively participated in volunteer work, engaging with children and contributing to various community projects. Beyond these outreach trips, our after-school sessions are filled with interactive workshops and activities designed to inspire creativity, teamwork, and personal growth. Members have the opportunity to learn new skills, collaborate on projects, and take part in planning future initiatives and trips. Through these activities, we aim to foster a sense of responsibility, empathy, and leadership, ensuring that every member not only develops personally but also makes a positive impact on the community.

What's next?

Looking ahead, our goal is to expand Illuminate to reach more schools, spreading the impact of SPES AURUM even further. We aim to continue creating meaningful experiences for students, inspiring leadership, creativity, and community involvement. By growing our network and reaching new communities, we hope to amplify the positive change we’ve started and empower even more young people to make a difference.
